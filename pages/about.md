**Centre County History Otherwise: Italian American Women** is a digital oral history collection documenting the lives, memory, and migration of Italian American women in Centre County, Pennsylvania. 

Through recorded interviews, the collection explores themes of immigration, family, faith, food, language and generational change, centering everyday experiences that are often left out of the official historical record. 

## About the Interviews
Each interview in this collection was conducted by Dr. Maria Truglio on Zoom over the course of several years, starting in 2022. We have transcribed and indexed themes, allowing visitors to search and browse across the collection by topic. 

## Project Team
This project is directed by Dr. Maria Truglio, Professor of Italian and Women’s, Gender, and Sexuality Studies, with support from Dr. Jennifer Isasi and the Digital Liberal Arts Research Initiative in the College of the Liberal Arts.
